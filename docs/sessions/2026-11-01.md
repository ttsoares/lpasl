## 2. Typography

**Shared text roles (`148f265`, `/impeccable typeset`).** `textRoles` in
`src/styles/tokens.ts`: `statement`, `itemTitle`, `lead`, `prose` and `meta`.
Every page uses them instead of one-off sizes. Editais (`Noticia.tsx`) open

dated, and chose option C. Archivo stays for headings and IBM Plex Mono for

Instrument's zero is wider, so `ch` widths no longer match the real line
length. Running-text widths are now in `em` (`layout.textWidth` = 33em, about
70 characters), and 13 widths were converted.
